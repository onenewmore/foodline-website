How to Choose the Right Packing Machine: A 5-Step Buyer's Guide

By the FOOD LINE engineering team · Updated 2026-11-06

Short answer: Choose a packing machine by answering five questions in order: what the product is, how much goes in each pack, how many packs per minute, what pack format, and what your site can supply (power, air, space).

1. Define your product

Product form decides the dosing system — the most important part of the machine.

  • Powder (milk powder, flour, coffee, spices): auger / screw filler such as our full-auto coffee powder sealing machine.
  • Free-flowing granules (sugar, rice, salt, seeds): volumetric cups or linear weigher on a vertical form fill seal machine.
  • Irregular solids (chips, nuts, candy, frozen food): multi-function vertical snack packer with a multihead weigher.
  • Liquids and pastes (sauce, honey, oil, shampoo): piston or rotor pump on a vertical sachet liquid packaging machine or bottle filling and capping line.
  • Single solid items (bread, biscuits, bars): horizontal flow wrap machine.

Also note particle size, density, stickiness, fragility and whether the product is corrosive or needs SUS316L.

2. Fix the weight or volume per pack

Small doses (1–50 g) point to stick pack or single-lane sachet machines; 50 g–5 kg to VFFS or automated pouch packaging equipment; 20–30 kg to a fully automatic granule packaging machine.

3. Calculate the speed you need

Divide daily output by effective running minutes. Example: 100,000 coffee sticks per 8-hour shift ≈ 210 sticks/min → a 6–8-lane stick-pack machine (25–40 sticks per lane per minute). For bulk bagging, below about 5,000 bags/day a semi-automatic line is enough; above 10,000 bags/day choose fully automatic.

5. Check your site

Measure ceiling height (weigher lines can be 3.6–4 m tall), floor space and access. Confirm voltage, phase and frequency, compressed air (usually 0.6–0.8 MPa), optional check weigher machine integration, and any explosion-proof requirement for chemical plants.

FAQ

How much ceiling height does a multihead weigher packing line need?

A complete Z-elevator + multihead weigher + VFFS line typically stands 3.6–4.0 m tall (4441 × 2000 × 3600 mm to 4700 × 2265 × 4000 mm across FL-420W to FL-820W), so measure door clearances and overhead height before ordering.

Can one packing machine run several different bag sizes?

Yes. On VFFS machines you swap the forming collar for different bag widths and adjust bag length on the touch screen; on 8-station premade-pouch machines bag width adjusts via touch screen within 80–350 mm.

Should a start-up buy a single-lane or multi-lane sachet machine?

A single-lane FL-240 produces 25–40 bags/min (about 12,000–19,000 sachets per 8-hour shift) with an 180 kg compact footprint. Step up to a 2–16 lane machine once daily demand exceeds that capacity.

How do I verify the recommended machine fits my product?

Send a product sample and target film or pouch; we test it on the recommended machine, share the running video and mail back finished sample packs before production.
