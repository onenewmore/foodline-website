FAQ about the Horizontal Flow Wrap Machine

Upper-film or lower-film flow wrapper?

Upper-film (standard) suits products in trays or with a defined top/bottom. Lower-film (back-pull) suits soft, sticky or irregular products like noodles, vegetables and bread, because the product lies on the film rather than on a belt.

Can it link to my production line?

Yes. An automatic sorting/feeding line receives products from your oven, cooling or moulding line, aligns them and feeds the wrapper — single-layer or stacked.
