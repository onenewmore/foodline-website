8-station rotary vs single-station and roll-film VFFS

Our 8-station rotary frames (FL-R200, FL-R250 and FL-R350) separate bag picking, date coding, zipper opening, two-stage filling, vibration settling, heat sealing, cooling and discharge into dedicated stations. No-bag / bag-not-opened interlocks stop filling and sealing when a pouch fails to open, saving printed bags and product. Single-station and horizontal premade-bag machines are also available for lower budgets, while high-volume pillow or gusset lines often choose a vertical form fill seal machine for lower roll-film cost. Read our VFFS vs premade pouch comparison.

Building a complete premade pouch line

A typical line links a Z-type bucket elevator, screw feeder or transfer pump upstream to a rotary turret, and discharges onto a finished-product conveyor with a check weigher machine, metal detector and rotary collecting table. Options include nitrogen flushing, degassing-valve applicators for roasted coffee, and SUS316L contact parts for salty or acidic sauces.

FAQ

How fast can operators switch pouch widths on a rotary premade pouch machine?

On the FL-R200, FL-R250 and FL-R350, bag width clamps adjust together from the touch screen in minutes within the 80–350 mm range, so one machine handles multiple retail pouch SKUs with minimal downtime.

What prevents product spilling if a premade pouch does not open?

Sensors check pouch pick-up and mouth opening before the filling station. Under the no-bag / bag-not-opened interlock, neither the doser nor the heat sealer cycles, preventing wasted product and messy jaws.

What power, air and floor space do rotary premade pouch lines require?

The rotary unit runs on 380 V or 220 V at 2.3–7.5 kW with 0.6–1.2 m³/min compressed air, and has a footprint of roughly 2300 × 1560 × 1460 mm (1600 kg gross weight), plus space for the overhead weigher or feeder.
