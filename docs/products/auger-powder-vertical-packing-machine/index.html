FAQ about the Powder VFFS with Auger Filler

How accurate is auger filling?

Typically ±1 % for fill weights above 100 g and about ±1–2 g below 100 g, depending on powder density and flowability.

Can it pack fine, dusty powders?

Yes. We add a dust-suction port, anti-drip auger and closed hopper; for very fine powder we recommend a vacuum feeder.
