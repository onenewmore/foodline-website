Coffee, Milk Tea & Sugar Stick Packing Machines

Single-serve sticks and sachets of instant coffee, 3-in-1 coffee, milk tea, creamer, sugar, cocoa and solid drinks.

Drink-mix sticks are packed on multi-lane back-seal machines: the film is slit into lanes, each lane forms, fills and seals its own stick, and all lanes are cut together. Output is lanes × 20–40 sticks per minute, so a 6-lane machine makes roughly 120–240 sticks per minute with one operator. Powdered coffee, milk tea and creamer use servo auger (screw) dosing; free-flowing sugar uses volumetric cups. For square drink-mix sachets choose a 4-side seal machine instead.

Which machine for which pack?

  • Slim stick packs (instant coffee, sugar, creamer, electrolyte): multi-lane stick pack machine FL-S300 / S600 / S900, 2–16 lanes, stick width 20–105 mm.
  • Square or rectangular sachets (3-in-1 coffee, milk tea): multi-lane 4-side seal machine FL-M500 / M700 / M980, sachet width 50–125 mm.
  • Low volume or start-up: single-lane FL-240B (back seal) at 25–40 sachets per minute.
  • Retail coffee beans or ground coffee: rotary premade-pouch machine with degassing-valve applicator and nitrogen flushing, or a jar filling line.

Packing only sugar? See sugar stick and sachet packing machines for cup-filler sizing, stick sizes and lane selection.

Dosing tips for coffee and milk-tea powders

3-in-1 coffee and milk-tea premixes contain sugar, creamer and coffee with different densities, and they absorb moisture. Use an agitated hopper so the mix stays uniform, keep the packing room dry, and choose a laminated film with a good moisture barrier (for example PET/AL/PE). Send us your premix and target weight and we test it on the machine before you order.

From sticks to boxes

Multi-lane machines can feed an automatic counting and boxing line, so finished sticks go straight into display boxes or cartons without manual counting.

FAQ

How many coffee sticks can one machine pack per minute?

Each lane runs 20–40 sticks per minute. A 4-lane machine packs about 80–160 sticks per minute, an 8-lane machine about 160–320, and a 16-lane machine up to about 640, depending on the powder and fill weight.

Can the same machine pack coffee and sugar?

Yes, if it is configured for both. Powder uses an auger filler and sugar uses volumetric cups, so we fit the dosing system for your main product and can supply a second dosing set for the other.
